===========================
Django 5.2.20 release notes
===========================

*Expected December 1, 2026*

Django 5.2.20 fixes one bug in 5.2.19.

Bugfixes
========

* Fixed a regression in Django 5.2.19 where a :exc:`RecursionError` from a
  GeoJSON value was not reraised as a
  :exc:`~django.core.exceptions.ValidationError` by the
  :class:`~django.contrib.gis.forms.GeometryField` form field
  (:ticket:`37491`).
