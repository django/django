==========================
Django 6.1.4 release notes
==========================

*Expected December 1, 2026*

Django 6.1.4 fixes several bugs in 6.1.3.

Bugfixes
========

* Fixed a regression in Django 5.2 that caused a crash of ``QuerySet.count()``
  and ``QuerySet.aggregate()`` on a queryset whose ``values()`` selected only
  aggregate annotations (:ticket:`37473`).
